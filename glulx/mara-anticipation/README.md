# Release 1261 — Mara Anticipation, Worry & Protective Initiative

Release 1261 makes Mara's lived history prospective.

Release 1260 established that specific events can acquire durable personal meaning: known recklessness can become concerned anger, intentional violence can become rupture, and later behavior can become repair evidence without deleting the harm. Release 1261 asks the next question: **can that history change what Mara does before the same danger happens again?**

## Product contract

This release adds authored prospective propositions, not a generic worry/emotion meter:

- Mara recognizes a specific Dam danger because it has already injured or endangered the Adventurer in shared history.
- She can speak before the next ladder commitment instead of waiting to narrate another injury.
- She truthfully checks equipment she actually possesses; she does not conjure safety, solve the puzzle, or manufacture a prepared route.
- The player's later real action becomes new evidence: the warning can be heeded or knowingly overridden.
- Heeding can produce relief because rescue became unnecessary.
- An unresolved rupture does **not** switch concern off, but concern also does not silently restore close physical cooperation. Mara can care whether the Adventurer falls while still refusing to put her body on the other end of the fall.

The causal chain is:

`remembered specific danger -> prospective appraisal -> spoken worry/protective initiative -> player choice -> heeded/overridden history -> later meaning`

## Qualification histories

The hosted qualifier establishes preconditions only with test verbs. The emotional/prospective consequences are exercised with real game actions:

1. **Heeded warning** — real movement from Reservoir South into the live Dam makes Mara warn before the ladder is touched; the player really drops the dangerous ballast and then descends.
2. **Knowingly overridden warning** — the same real arrival produces the warning, then the player really attempts the unchanged overloaded descent; Release 1260 remains authoritative about refusing to become a reset-button rescue.
3. **Concern during rupture** — the player really enters the Dam where Mara is keeping distance; she warns because the danger still matters, then the existing rupture authority refuses close-contact backstop when the unsafe descent is attempted.

## Non-goals

- no global worry, affection, trust, or relationship score
- no generic autonomous planning engine
- no companion puzzle solver
- no fake rope state or magically prepared safety equipment
- no erasure of Release 1260 rupture/boundary semantics
- no repeated warning spam after the prospective fact has already been spoken

This release stays stacked on Release 1260. Do not merge without the explicit merge whistle.
