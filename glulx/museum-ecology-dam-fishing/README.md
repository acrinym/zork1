# Release 1239 — Museum Ecology and Dam Fishing

Release 1239 returns to the museum and makes its first natural-history display playable.

## Player loop

1. Find the jointed ash fishing rod and water-filled field jar beside the Living Room museum.
2. Carry both physical tools to the River Frigid at the base of Flood Control Dam #3.
3. `FISH` or `CAST ROD`.
4. Catch one real dam silverfin into the real specimen jar.
5. Examine the animal to see the variety produced by the existing dam state:
   - normal river conditions produce a narrow river silverfin;
   - `LOW-TIDE` produces a broad-tailed, gate-scarred spillway silverfin.
6. Choose what the museum receives:
   - `RELEASE SILVERFIN` at the dam records the observation and returns the animal alive;
   - carry the specimen to the Living Room and `EXHIBIT SILVERFIN` to place the actual fish in the flowing Waters of the Empire case.
7. `CATALOG WATERS` or `READ SILVERFIN PLAQUE` to inspect the evidence and provenance.
8. Taking the fish out of the case physically empties the living exhibit while preserving only what was genuinely observed.

## Fishing rod

The rod is jointed ash with a cork grip, fine green line, and a narrow brass reel. Its museum tag identifies it as field equipment. The reel is deliberately overbuilt in the traditional Great Underground Empire manner.

## Museum truth

The museum does not create a specimen when the player releases one. It displays a field record instead. When the player donates the fish, the real object moves into the case. The plaque distinguishes:

- no evidence;
- a released and registered living observation;
- a real specimen currently on display;
- a known specimen currently absent from the case.

## Boundaries

- no generic fishing engine or procedural species generator;
- no aquarium checklist or fish currency;
- no remote donation or copied object;
- no invented tide controller;
- no duplicate catch while the known specimen still exists;
- no audit framework or recursive planning machinery.
