# Attic Playback — Release 1227 Candidate

## Product boundary

Release `1227` extends qualified Release `1226` with bounded, period-authentic playback of meaningful events already earned by this expedition.

The Attic gains:

- `PLAYBACK-PRINTER-01` — continuous-feed curated transcript;
- `PLAYBACK-CASSETTE-02` — textual cassette with speaker labels, pauses, hiss, and environmental cues;
- `PLAYBACK-INCIDENT-03` — incident scene index;
- `PLAYBACK-ACTOR-04` — actor scene index;
- `PLAYBACK-PLACE-05` — place scene index;
- `PLAYBACK-CHRONOLOGY-06` — deterministic first-capture order;
- `PLAYBACK-FORENSIC-07` — bounded twelve-event forensic strip.

## What is captured

The recorder observes only consequential state already established by the canonical game and earlier qualified layers:

- house return and Attic use;
- individually earned forest evidence;
- Dam diagnosis, refused or failed interactions, and repair;
- Hades ceremony stages, wrong-order consequences, and completion;
- player-specific troll, cyclops, and thief evidence;
- complete regional synthesis.

Each event is recorded once. Ordinary movement, parser chatter, typos, repeated observations, and unseen events are not stored.

## Curated, not omniscient

Printer command echoes are normalized labels such as `[CURATED ACTION] EXAMINE THE DAM CONTROL PANEL`. They are not represented as the player's exact historical wording.

Cassette speech is contextual paraphrase, not invented verbatim dialogue. Missing scenes remain absent.

## No time travel

Playback review is non-turning for threat and timer machinery. Every guarded read snapshots and verifies:

- location;
- score;
- active Shadow threat timer;
- troll, cyclops, and thief location;
- parser pronoun;
- physical record custody.

Playback may print text and mark that the archive was reviewed. It cannot replay actors, move objects, change score, open routes, advance timers, or repair evidence.

## Capacity and persistence

The sequence stores at most twelve unique consequential events. The native save image retains:

- captured-event mask;
- deterministic sequence;
- record indexes;
- filing state;
- prior parser pronoun;
- exact physical custody.

The qualification route saves, deliberately removes state and records, and restores without post-restore repair.
