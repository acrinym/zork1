# Release 1276 — Mara Field Guidance & Earned Clues

Release 1276 makes Mara materially useful as a mappist and field partner without turning her into an omniscient hint terminal.

## Product behavior

Mara may guide only from facts she can truthfully possess: places she physically visited, evidence she inspected, routes she investigated, facts she was told, and object placements she personally witnessed.

The first authored field chapter uses Release 1273's Western Backcountry. After the shared Dam survey has established real expedition company, Mara can accompany the Adventurer through the backcountry. Her field notebook records only selected places she actually reaches.

At Fox Run, Mara can independently notice the existing animal-route evidence. This remains private Mara knowledge until she communicates it. Only then does the existing Release 1273 fox-route authority become known to the Adventurer; Release 1276 does not create a second shortcut state.

At Basin Overlook, Mara can observe that a warm wet descent exists without claiming the unseen Warmrain topology. If the Adventurer continues south while Mara stays behind, her map records that exact boundary as a route she did not enter.

If Mara personally witnesses the Adventurer drop an ordinary carried object, she remembers that object and location. Later cache recall checks the object's real current location. If it has moved, Mara reports only the last placement she witnessed and explicitly refuses to invent its new location.

## Difficulty composition

Structural difficulty affects when Mara volunteers a finding, never what she knows.

- Forgiving: she immediately communicates the fox-route conclusion after checking it.
- Classic: she records the discovery and leaves a conversational opening; the player can ask what she found.
- Exacting: she records the discovery without volunteering it; direct questioning still returns the same earned fact.

## Authority boundaries

- existing Mara state, movement, personhood, causal biography, capability, lived-feeling, and anticipation remain authoritative;
- Release 1273 wilderness geography and `WB-FOX-TRAIL-SEEN` remain the real shortcut/Adventurer-knowledge authority;
- Release 1269 remains the structural-difficulty authority;
- ordinary object location remains current cache truth;
- Release 1276 adds zero legacy VM globals;
- its fixed field-guidance table owns only selected Mara-specific visit/knowledge/cache facts.

There is no generic pathfinder, quest-marker system, walkthrough database, universal map graph, automatic clue registry, invisible inventory tracker, generic NPC knowledge engine, or companion AI.

## Qualification contract

Qualification must prove with natural player commands that Mara:

1. physically follows into approved backcountry terrain only after the existing Dam-survey prerequisite;
2. independently discovers the fox clue without automatically granting it to the Adventurer in Classic/Exacting;
3. can communicate that clue and thereby compose with the existing wilderness shortcut authority;
4. distinguishes confirmed backcountry ground from the merely observed Warmrain descent;
5. records a route she personally did not enter without learning its far-side topology;
6. remembers one witnessed cache, then admits loss of current-location knowledge after the object moves;
7. changes volunteer cadence across Forgiving/Classic/Exacting while preserving the same underlying fact;
8. leaves earlier Mara and wilderness authorities intact.
