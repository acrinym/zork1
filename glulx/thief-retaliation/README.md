# Release 1255 — Thief Retaliation & Sabotage

Release 1255 makes deliberate treatment of the canonical thief matter without replacing his roaming, treasure, combat, or loot behavior.

- Provoking the thief creates one bounded persistent **grudge boolean**, never a grindable attitude meter.
- Future authored encounters can become personal: he may dump the brown sack's contents, selectively steal a useful non-sacred tool, or reveal that the theft is about inconvenience rather than value.
- Carrying visible steel is an earned deterrent: he can abandon the ambush rather than act omnisciently or suicidally.
- A valuable gift explicitly settles the personal account while leaving the thief a thief.
- Sabotage is reversible through ordinary Zork actions; selectively stolen tools remain in the canonical thief/booty custody path.
- Death clears the personal grudge; canonical thief combat, roaming, Treasure Room defense, stiletto recovery, theft, and loot deposit remain authoritative.
