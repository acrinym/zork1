# Creative Natural Play — post-Release 1244

This branch deliberately plays the current enlarged Zork as a player rather than designing the next feature train.

The observation pass starts from the exact merged Release 1244 story and combines old and new mechanics in ways their original release qualifications did not necessarily combine: House fixtures and food, rest, correspondence, troll combat and absurd alternates, museum ecology, Dam fishing, Mara travel and House history, and odd parser experiments.

The rule is simple: **observe first, fix only demonstrated failures, then replay.**

No test-only setup verb is added to production. No generic QA framework is introduced. The transcripts are ordinary Glulxe sessions beginning at West of House.

This train is intentionally unmerged until Justin gives a later merge whistle.
