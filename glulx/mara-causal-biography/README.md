# Release 1258 — Mara Causal Biography & Shared Danger

Release 1258 proves that Mara can make a later, visibly different decision because of **specific lived history**, without consulting a global approval/romance/trust score for that decision.

## Player-facing chapter

The existing Flood Control Dam #3 maintenance ladder becomes a shared-danger chapter built from already-shipped physical authorities:

- the real open-sluice ladder hazard from Release 1253;
- Mara's one physical measured field rope;
- her exact body and location;
- her existing warned-and-ignored blue-circuit history;
- ordinary player movement and object custody.

When asked to descend the dangerous ladder, Mara can slip, scrape a palm, and injure her shoulder. Her rope physically lands in the Adventurer's custody.

The Adventurer can:

- make one explicit, world-grounded promise to return that rope before moving away;
- rescue Mara from the ladder;
- return the exact rope through ordinary `GIVE FIELD ROPE TO MARA`;
- keep or break the promise through actual movement rather than dialogue selection;
- abandon her in active peril, which remains a separate biographical fact from promise-breaking.

The injury persists and is visible on later examination.

## Reciprocal rescue

Later, the Adventurer can face the **same** overloaded open-sluice ladder hazard that Release 1253 already knows how to make lethal.

Mara's response is reason-driven:

- prior abandonment or a broken rope promise gives her a concrete reason to refuse the improvisation;
- warned-and-ignored Dam history gives her a concrete reason to stop a reckless repeat;
- if the Adventurer actually rescued Mara **and returned her exact rope**, that later evidence can justify Mara taking the other end and catching the Adventurer's slip.

The old ignored warning is not erased. In the earned-reciprocity history, Mara explicitly remembers both facts.

The new 1258 ladder decision **does not read** `MARA-SLOT-TRUST`, `MARA-SLOT-RESPECT`, or `MARA-SLOT-SAFETY`. Those older fields remain untouched for compatibility with already-shipped behavior.

## Private experience and disclosure

After catching the Adventurer, Mara descends separately. On the way down she notices an old survey punch beneath a lower retaining bolt.

That observation belongs to Mara first.

She rejoins the Adventurer without automatically narrating the discovery. Only a later question about the ladder causes her to disclose what she saw and why she waited to call it evidence.

This is the first explicit proof that Mara can have a bounded, player-relevant experience whose acquisition and disclosure are separate events.

## Causal biography

Release 1258 advances Mara's table state to schema 6 with named propositions for this authored chapter:

- warning ignored;
- Mara rescued;
- exact rope returned;
- promise broken;
- peril abandoned;
- Mara rescued the Adventurer;
- ladder peril and persistent injury;
- rope promise state;
- private ladder discovery/disclosure;
- pending physical reunion.

These are not a generic psychology engine. They are compact, inspectable reasons attached to things that actually happened.

Schema 3, 4, and 5 histories migrate forward without erasing previous House, Dam, hostility, evidence, or witnessed-death history.

## Product boundary
