# Release 1260 — Mara Lived Feeling, Rupture & Repair

Release 1260 completes the current Mara-focused sequence by filling in the missing middle of her causal biography:

> event fact → Mara's appraisal → carried meaning → current feeling → choice → new history

This release authors Mara Tallow. It does not add a generic emotion engine, relationship meter, romance system, NPC-memory framework, or procedural apology system.

## Player-facing chapters

### You knew better

The existing Dam maintenance-ladder danger remains authoritative. Once Mara and the Adventurer have reciprocal rescue history, a later knowingly repeated overloaded descent can still end with Mara catching the Adventurer and the real Dam authority producing a bruised, soaked injury.

Release 1260 records what that later choice means to Mara: the Adventurer already knew what the danger could do and chose it anyway. Mara helps because the Adventurer is hurt while also becoming angry at the chosen recklessness. With earned reciprocal history, later conversation can expose fear and relief underneath the anger.

Repeating the same reckless setup yet again is not consequence-free. Mara refuses to become the machinery that makes the same deliberate danger safe forever.

### You meant to hurt me

Release 1245 remains the physical-safety authority: Mara steps clear of a direct attack rather than becoming an ordinary damage target or obtainable object.

Release 1260 adds the missing causal interpretation. An intentional attack records that the Adventurer chose Mara as the danger's target. Mara treats that as betrayal, creates physical distance, leaves automatic following, and refuses close cooperation while the rupture is unresolved.

`APOLOGIZE TO MARA` can acknowledge the event. It cannot erase it or immediately restore the prior relationship state.

### Repair without erasure

Repair requires new lived evidence:

1. the Adventurer acknowledges the attack;
2. Mara asks for space;
3. the Adventurer actually leaves the shared location rather than ordering Mara back into reach;
4. later, at the same real Dam danger, the Adventurer chooses a materially safer setup;
5. Mara notices the changed behavior and can choose working proximity again.

The intentional attack remains historical after repair. So do the apology, respected boundary, repair evidence, and Mara's later renewed choice.

## State contract

Schema 8 adds named propositions only:

- known-risk injury;
- Mara aided the reckless injury;
- anger at repeated recklessness;
- fear beneath that anger was revealed;
- intentional harm;
- betrayal meaning;
- current rupture open;
- apology acknowledged;
- requested boundary respected;
- repair evidence witnessed;
- rupture repaired.

`MARA-SLOT-RUPTURE-OPEN` is a current carried meaning, not a score. A future intentional attack can reopen rupture without deleting the fact that an earlier rupture was once repaired.

Legacy trust/respect/safety scalars remain outside the Release 1260 decision authority.
