# Completed Expedition Archive Contract

## Purpose

Train 12 closes the House of Records program by sealing completed playthroughs as physical, bounded archive records. It does not add universal telemetry, raw command logging, cloud state, or mutable replay.

## Completion authority

Canonical `WON-FLAG` is the only completion gate. No master expedition record, completed box, comparison, or export may appear before genuine victory.

Previously earned partial records remain valid before victory and are never discarded merely because the master record is unavailable.

## Chronology authority

The existing bounded playback sequence is the chronology source. It retains consequential House, forest, Dam, Hades, actor, failure, repair, and synthesis events in established order. Train 12 copies that sequence into a sealed box; it does not reconstruct a universal transcript.

Canonical `DEATHS` is retained separately so deaths are not inferred from prose or omitted from the final record.

## Final-state authority

A sealed summary may report only state already established by the run, including:

- score and death count;
- bounded actor and area outcomes;
- mechanisms and object fates through existing records and custody;
- correspondence and archive evidence;
- house incident history, repaired conditions, and security.

Missing evidence remains explicitly missing.

## Separate-box contract

Each completed history occupies one physical Attic banker box with its own master file, chronology roll, and final summary. A second completed history occupies a second box.

No command merges boxes, overwrites the first with the second, or treats contradictory outcomes as one run.

## Comparison contract

Comparison requires two sealed boxes. It may compare score, deaths, consequential event masks, actor encounters, area outcomes, object and house evidence, correspondence, chronology, repairs, and security.

It must not reveal unseen commands, routes, ceremony order, solution text, or outcomes absent from both records.

## Export and migration

`EXPEDITION-EXPORT-01` is a deterministic human-readable receipt carrying an explicit schema version and sealed-box mask. Migration conservatively rematerializes exact physical records from native state; it does not synthesize unavailable history.

## Persistence

Native save and restore preserve sealed masks, snapshots, per-box chronology arrays, physical object custody, comparison, export, schema, and event receipts without post-restore repair.

## Program boundary

Train 12 closes the existing eight expedition beads and the twelve-train, ninety-six-bead House of Records program. It creates no sub-beads or replacement planning hierarchy.
