# Flathead Fair time, schedule, and season

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Daily lifecycle

The fair is not static scenery. Its authored daily states should include:

1. setup / pre-opening;
2. opening morning;
3. late morning;
4. midday crowd peak;
5. afternoon contests and performances;
6. dusk / lighting transition;
7. evening social period;
8. closing sequence;
9. after-hours / mostly empty grounds.

Different stalls, performers, crowds, rides, NPC schedules, and dialogue may change by state.

## Event lifecycle

The fair itself should recur. A fuller calendar authority may eventually support:

- setup days;
- several open fair days;
- closing night;
- teardown;
- absent/off-season grounds;
- later return.

The exact recurrence interval remains open. Annual is attractive because it gives records and memories emotional weight, but implementation must match the broader calendar/aging model rather than forcing a fake year counter.

## Scheduling rules

- Missing a performance must not break required progression.
- A contest may recur at a later scheduled time/day.
- Closing must be legible to the player.
- Shops should close individually where appropriate rather than every actor disappearing on one global tick.
- Evening should have authored content, not merely darker descriptions.
- After-hours grounds should be materially quieter and potentially stranger.

## Long-term continuity

Later fairs may change vendors, prices, attractions, prize stock, records, performers, and NPC ages/status. Variation should be authored and stateful, not procedurally generated content.
