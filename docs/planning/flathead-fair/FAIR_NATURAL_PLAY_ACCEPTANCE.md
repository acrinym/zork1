# Flathead Fair natural-play acceptance

**Status:** STABLE FOR PLANNING  
**Purpose:** future qualification contract; no implementation is authorized yet

## Canonical non-interference

A release that introduces the fair must prove that ordinary canonical Zork play still works when the player never enters the fair.

At minimum:

- existing House/Clearing/Canyon exits remain correct;
- `CLEARING` still leads west/east/north/south exactly as before;
- the new northeast fair spur is additive;
- canonical forest barriers remain intact;
- no required treasure moves into fair geography;
- fair opening/closing cannot block a canonical route;
- weather/fair schedules cannot make a required canonical solution randomly unavailable.

## Fair approach acceptance

Natural play should prove:

1. reach canonical `CLEARING` by an ordinary route;
2. observe a fair cue only when relevant fair state makes one plausible;
3. travel northeast to `FAIR-ROAD` without altering existing exits;
4. enter the fairgrounds without paying general admission;
5. leave again and resume canonical surface play.

## Free-entry acceptance

A player with zero spendable zorkmids must still be able to enter grounds, walk public routes, talk to NPCs, watch ordinary public activity, accompany Mara, inspect products/menus/signage and leave safely.

They may correctly be unable to buy paid products/services.

## Commerce acceptance

Representative qualification should cover:

- inspect a priced product;
- ask its seller how much it costs;
- buy it with sufficient zorkmids;
- fail contextually with insufficient funds;
- preserve physical object identity after purchase;
- ask a vendor about their own product and receive specific knowledge;
- ask an unrelated NPC and receive only knowledge they plausibly have;
- preserve prize-ticket versus zorkmid versus ride-strip boundaries.

## Product-identification acceptance

The first locked case is the 4-zm pear-lime fizz:

1. encounter it as a `large drink` without exact flavor knowledge;
2. ask Tomas Quince what flavor it is;
3. learn that it is pear-lime fizz;
4. retain the same physical drink object;
5. allow Mara's answer to depend on what she has actually smelled/tasted/heard;
6. prevent unrelated NPCs from receiving omniscient recipe knowledge;
7. support useful follow-up rather than replaying first-contact prose forever.

## Food acceptance

Elephant-ear qualification should cover:

- cinnamon-sugar baseline price of 5 zm;
- Mabel Rusk knowing ingredients/preparation/price;
- eating/carrying/giving/sharing according to food/object authority;
- Mara choosing whether she wants/eats/shares one rather than being forced by the purchase;
- Mara's apple-topped preference being character behavior rather than a guaranteed purchase script;
- persistence of a meaningful shared-food memory only through personal memory authority.

## NPC acceptance

Named NPCs must not behave as stationary generic terminals.

Representative routes should sample both the 12-person core and focused secondary cast for:

- job-specific knowledge;
- correct ignorance outside role;
- schedule state and visible transitions;
- closing/weather behavior;
- memory of a prior interaction where persistence is supported;
- character-specific opinion rather than database prose;
- refusal, deception or uncertainty where legitimately authored.

Specific future examples:

- Kester Vane can lie/minimize about his own booth without becoming omniscient;
- Hettie Bramm can rule out mundane mechanical causes without declaring a supernatural truth;
- Sella Birch can comment on craft/material evidence without certifying historical provenance;
- Cassa Reed can compete and beat the Adventurer or Mara while Silas remains official verifier;
- Lysa Marr and Tavin Roe remain separate people with separate location/preferences.

## Games acceptance

Qualification should prove mechanic diversity rather than merely eight descriptions around the same random result.

Representative routes should include:

- one learnable low-RNG booth such as Ring Stand;
- one deterministic observation/timing activity such as Target Gallery or What's Missing? after layout commitment;
- one bounded-chance competition;
- one suspicious shell-game evidence route;
- a record/result path that distinguishes current Fair Office state from durable archive graduation.

### Shell-game fairness acceptance

For F-04:

1. play/observe Kester's booth without omniscient prior knowledge;
2. notice/test a concrete inconsistency;
3. inspect the cup/pea state closely enough to expose tack-wax evidence where the route supports it;
4. preserve physical evidence rather than replacing it with a `CHEATING=TRUE` revelation;
5. allow Kester to deny/minimize according to his knowledge/motive;
6. present evidence through the current association/Fair Office path;
7. prove that durable historical treatment occurs only after the authored adjudication/graduation rules are satisfied.

Hidden author-side answer changing is an automatic acceptance failure.

## Fishing acceptance

Representative fishing qualification should include:

1. rent or use a rod;
2. ask Silas for useful advice without exposing internal probability tables;
3. fish meaningfully different position/bait contexts;
4. catch an ordinary fish;
5. encounter deterministic-fixture junk in a test route;
6. establish a hooked/catch fact and prove later inspection reads the same committed specimen;
7. weigh a derby-eligible fish;
8. prove Mara or Cassa can independently compete and beat the Adventurer;
9. distinguish an ordinary catch from a verified standing-record result;
10. prove no rare catch is required for canonical progression.

A record-class fish remains an exceptional specimen of a real species, not a `LEGENDARY FISH` spawn class.

## Mara acceptance

Mara remains autonomous and character-specific at the fair.

Representative tests should include cases where she can:

- accept or refuse a suggestion;
- prefer the Observation Wheel at dusk/evening rather than treating all phases identically;
- choose/notice her dragon carousel preference where available;
- initiate or decline a game/fishing/dance activity;
- beat the Adventurer in a supported activity;
- refuse repeated shell-game payment after credible cheating evidence;
- choose food/drink according to her authored preferences and current knowledge;
- become bored/annoyed when the player keeps pushing a clearly refused/repetitive activity;
- remember a semantic prior-fair event where broader recurrence authority supports it;
- avoid relationship behavior unsupported by current consent/state.

There is no hidden fair-only romance override or player-ego protection roll.

## Lantern Table / adult-social acceptance

Representative evening play should prove:

- Tomas leaves his daytime Food Row role at DUSK through visible world state and operates The Lantern Table in Dance Pavilion;
- ordinary pavilion/social entry remains free and requires no drink purchase;
- existing Lantern Table products use the ordinary zorkmid economy;
- an edge table is quieter public context, not a magic private room;
- communal tables/counter areas remain socially public;
- Mara may accept/refuse dancing or food/drink;
- Lysa/Tavin may be present separately;
- closing gives a real last-service/final-piece sequence rather than instant despawn;
- AFTER-HOURS does not become an automatically open romance bar;
- no alcohol/intoxication behavior exists unless broader authority explicitly owns it.

## Fair Office / House of Records acceptance

The fair must prove the difference between **current administration** and **durable historical archive**.

Representative qualification should cover:

1. reach Fair Office & Prize Hall and ask Ada about a current schedule, permit, entry/result, lost property or current incident;
2. receive useful current-office information when she plausibly has it;
3. ask for an older historical fact whose evidence lives upstairs;
4. have Ada identify/direct toward the archive source rather than magically return remote file contents;
5. use the existing House of Records physical/review/cross-reference authority;
6. preserve provenance when an old program, current program, Ephraim memory and Frobozz claim disagree;
7. allow a documented contradiction to remain unresolved when evidence does not resolve it.

### Graduation acceptance

At least one future route should prove **non-graduation**:

- complete an ordinary booth attempt or ordinary fish catch;
- close/advance the relevant current state;
- confirm that no permanent House of Records file appears merely because the activity happened.

At least one route should prove **graduation**:

- establish a standing record, annual public result or significant adjudicated incident;
- retain the current Fair Office source/provenance;
- pass through legitimate closeout/adjudication;
- expose the resulting durable record through existing House of Records authority.

### Correction acceptance

A result correction/disqualification route should prove:

- the original result remains attributable to its original source;
- the later correcting authority/evidence is separately present;
- the previous result is marked superseded/disputed/disqualified rather than erased;
- review/cross-reference can expose the relationship where existing archive authority supports it.

### Physical evidence remains physical

For the old pond-token path:

- the token remains the same physical object;
- archived program/map/concession evidence may provide context;
- filing/cross-referencing does not replace the token with an abstract archive flag;
- Vera, Sella, Ephraim, Ada and archival sources remain bounded/provenance-bearing and may disagree.

### Privacy acceptance

Qualification must explicitly prevent institutional memory from becoming surveillance:

- Mara remembering a private wheel ride, kiss, conversation or elephant-ear dispute does not create Fair Office or House of Records paperwork by itself;
- private NPC thoughts/preferences do not appear in archive records unless independently documented through an authored public/documentary event;
- the archive cannot answer private relationship questions merely because it persists world history.

## RNG and SAVE/RESTORE acceptance

The product contract is now explicit and must be qualified as written.

### Runtime fixture

The active lineage targets Glulx 3.1.3. Native deterministic RNG fixtures may launch the pinned Glulxe `56ab8743bab565de307bd892c555d8d8897ed517` with a known nonzero:

```text
--rngseed <seed>
```

No new fair-specific seed parser verb is required.

### Committed-fact route

At least one future route must:

1. use a known interpreter seed;
2. trigger a random fair fact at its authored commitment boundary;
3. inspect/record the fact;
4. SAVE;
5. advance play;
6. RESTORE;
7. inspect the fact again;
8. prove the **story-state fact** did not reroll.

This tests the fair's state commitment, not a false assumption that Glulx saves RNG internal state.

### Pre-commit route

A separate qualification note/route should establish that an outcome not yet committed at the save point is **not promised** to replay identically after RESTORE, because player-managed Glulx SAVE/RESTORE does not include RNG internal state.

The fair must not:

- punish the player for restoring;
- store hidden non-saveable anti-save history;
- globally reseed production RNG to force a result;
- reroll an already visible/committed fact on repeated `LOOK` or re-entry.

### Multiple-seed variation

Where useful, separate deterministic routes with two known process seeds should prove that an optional authored random family can legitimately vary while remaining within its allowed table/mechanics.

Qualification asserts semantic outcomes/state, not raw PRNG numbers.

## Fair-life acceptance

Representative natural-play histories should ultimately cover:

- daytime wandering;
- buying/eating an elephant ear;
- pear-lime product-question path;
- one ride;
- one game;
- fishing;
- a scheduled performance/event;
- dusk/night transition;
- Lantern Table social use;
- closing/exit;
- Mara agency/refusal/initiative;
- one current Fair Office interaction;
- one historical cross-reference into the existing House of Records when appropriate.

A later annual-repeat history is required only when the broader calendar can honestly support a later fair. Do not invent a fake year counter solely to satisfy this test.

## Time/weather acceptance

When Release 1307 time/weather authority is available to consume:

- fair phases alter only authored schedules/attractions;
- a weather closure has visible cause;
- wind-sensitive machinery follows named operating authority;
- rain changes activity/coverage/crowd behavior rather than blindly cancelling the fair;
- paid services closed before use follow authored refund/credit policy;
- no weather event destroys required fair or canonical progression;
- off-season/closed grounds remain coherent geography rather than disappearing.

## Persistence acceptance

Where broader calendar/recurrence permits a later fair, prove separately that:

- selected documentary records persist through existing House of Records authority;
- owned prizes remain physical property;
- vendor/NPC memories persist through personal/world memory authority;
- Mara memories persist through Mara authority;
- temporary stock/crowds/routine office paperwork reset according to lifecycle;
- documentary and personal memory do not collapse into one storage model.

## No cheat leakage

Qualification helpers must not create a new production fair-debug surface.

Using the pinned interpreter's process-level `--rngseed` in hosted/native qualification is acceptable because it does not add a player-facing fair command.

The repository's historical `#RANDOM` debug command remains under its existing broader ownership and is not a required fair interface.

## No completion requirement

Qualification does not require exhausting every prize, fish, NPC, product or miniquest. The fair is a place to revisit, not a `100%` checklist.

## Acceptance maturity

This document is stable enough to define the future natural-play contract. Exact transcript commands, fixtures, artifact hashes and release-specific paths remain implementation-train evidence and must not be invented during planning.
