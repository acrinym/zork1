# Flathead Fair natural-play acceptance

**Status:** DESIGNING

This is the future qualification contract, not an implementation test script yet.

## Canonical compatibility

A release containing the fair must prove:

- canonical Zork progression remains solvable without entering the fair;
- existing exits are not stolen/rebound by fair geography;
- fair closing does not trap required progression;
- no canonical treasure/object is randomly deleted or required to gamble;
- leaving and re-entering remains coherent.

## Commerce acceptance

- zorkmid purchases charge the intended amount;
- bought objects are real and persist correctly;
- ticket/prize currency cannot silently substitute for zorkmids;
- sold-out/insufficient-money behavior is contextual;
- Mara may purchase independently where designed.

## Knowledge acceptance

For representative products, prove that:

- partial descriptions can become identified;
- vendor answers reflect vendor knowledge;
- Mara answers reflect her actual knowledge;
- ignorant NPCs can redirect correctly;
- no generic response replaces a known authored answer.

## Fair-life acceptance

Representative natural-play histories should cover:

- daytime wandering;
- buying/eating an elephant ear;
- one ride;
- one game;
- fishing;
- a scheduled performance/event;
- dusk/night transition;
- closing/exit;
- at least one return visit;
- Mara agency and refusal/initiative;
- controlled RNG determinism/replay behavior.

## Persistence acceptance

Where the broader calendar permits a later fair, prove that selected records, owned prizes, and memory events survive while temporary stock/crowds reset.

## No-cheat law

Test helpers may establish expensive state in test builds, but production must not expose qualification verbs or alternate world-state machinery.
