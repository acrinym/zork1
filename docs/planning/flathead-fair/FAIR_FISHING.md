# Flathead Fair fishing

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Goal

Fishing should be a real optional hobby, not `>FISH` followed by one random line.

## Inputs

A bounded fishing model may consider:

- bait/lure;
- rod/line class;
- fishing position;
- time of day;
- weather;
- patience / number of attempts;
- fish behavior tables;
- current derby state.

Avoid a universal ecosystem simulator.

## Catch classes

- common fish;
- uncommon species;
- tiny or embarrassing catches;
- large/record fish;
- junk such as boots or discarded objects;
- lost personal items;
- rare curiosities;
- extremely rare impossible/strange catches that remain authored.

## Physicality

Caught fish and junk should use real object identity where practical. Records refer to the actual catch event, not a detached score.

## Fishing derby

A scheduled competition can provide named rival anglers, weigh-in, records, ribbons/tickets, and recurring reputation. Mara may enter independently and can beat the Adventurer.

## Equipment philosophy

Better equipment creates different affordances or reliability, not a simple `BEST ROD = BEST FISH` ladder.

## RNG boundary

Fishing is a good home for controlled randomness, but rare catches must not be required for canonical progression or an essential relationship outcome.
