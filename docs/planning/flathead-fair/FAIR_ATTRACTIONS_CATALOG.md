# Flathead Fair attractions catalog

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Flagship attractions

### Observation wheel

A major social and scenic attraction. Supports changing views by time/weather, Mara conversations, distant-landmark observation, evening privacy, and rare mechanical interruptions such as a temporary stop. It must not become a disaster machine.

### Carousel

Uses Zork-native carved creatures rather than generic horses. Mount choice should be examinable and occasionally meaningful. Possible forms include grue, dragon, cyclops, sea creature, unicorn, or stranger local beasts where lore permits.

### Swing / flying-chair ride

Wind and weather may matter. Loose objects/clothing can have authored reactions without turning into universal physics.

### Scenic Great Underground Empire ride

A deliberately artificial tourist version of locations/history the Adventurer may actually know. Comedy comes from inaccurate civic propaganda and the gap between fake exhibit and lived reality.

### House of Mirrors

A deep parser attraction supporting:

- convex/concave mirrors;
- endless-reflection effects;
- ordinary and strange reflections;
- navigation confusion;
- touch/knock/wave/smile/show-object interactions;
- Mara reactions;
- hidden or rare anomalous behavior.

Most mirrors should remain mundane so genuinely strange behavior retains force.

## Attraction rules

- Rides must have reasons to repeat beyond identical prose.
- Mara can accept, refuse, suggest, or initiate.
- Weather/time can alter availability and experience.
- Mechanical failures are rare authored incidents, not random punishment.
- No attraction may gate canonical Zork completion.
- No Gato analogue or giant robot-animal combat attraction.
