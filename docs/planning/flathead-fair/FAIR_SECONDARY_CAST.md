## Purpose

The initial 12-person core roster established the fair's operating spine. This pass fills the specific missing people already demanded by the existing stories and systems.

The planning roster is now **20 named fair NPCs total**:

- 12 core people in `FAIR_NPC_ROSTER.md`;
- 8 secondary people here.

This document resolves the previous placeholder language for the dubious shell-game operator, maintenance specialist, artisan, child/family characters, recurring couple and rival angler. Where an older focused document still says `future` or `to be named` for one of those roles, the identity in this document is authoritative.

These are not eight new quest dispensers. Each exists because they create a distinct knowledge boundary, activity, continuity pattern or evidence seam.

## 13. Kester Vane — shell/cup operator

**Primary space:** Games Row.  
**Role:** operator of the Shell / Cup Game and principal person in F-04, `The Shell Game Is Not Merely Difficult`.

Kester is practiced, personable and defensive about the difference between `difficult` and `unfair`. He does not advertise himself as a villain and should be capable of ordinary conversation that is not about cheating.

### Physical fairness authority

For the initial F-04 configuration, Kester's paid game is genuinely dishonest in a way the world can expose.

One of his cups has a **thin tack-wax patch beneath the inner lip**. During a paid sequence, the pea can adhere briefly to that hidden patch, allowing Kester to lift or transfer the cup while creating the false impression that the pea remained on the table. The mechanism leaves concrete evidence:

- one cup has a slightly different interior feel/weight;
- close inspection can reveal the tack-wax patch or residue;
- the pea can acquire matching residue;
- repeated observation can establish that the suspicious transfer occurs under conditions Kester controls;
- the equipment can be compared with an honest demonstration or Jonas Pell's account of fair-game practice.

The author never secretly moves the answer after the player has made a correct observation. If Kester cheats, the physical cup/pea state explains how.

Kester may deny, minimize, refuse inspection, claim ordinary wear, or close the booth when scrutiny becomes uncomfortable. Those are character responses, not proof by themselves.

### Knowledge boundary

Kester knows his own equipment, sequence, prices, customer patterns and what he personally did. He may lie about those facts when self-protection motivates it.

He does not know every fair record, Mara's private opinions, House of Records contents, or incidents he did not witness.

A formally established cheating incident begins as current Fair Office paperwork. Only an adjudicated, historically significant case may later become a durable House of Records candidate.

### Expected day

- PREOPEN: Games Row setup and equipment check;
- OPENING through AFTERNOON: operates the shell/cup game;
- DUSK/EVENING: may remain open with the other lit games unless sanctioned/closed;
- CLOSING: stops new paid rounds and secures the booth;
- AFTER-HOURS: normally absent.

An F-04 sanction can override this schedule visibly. He does not teleport away because a flag changed.

## 14. Hettie Bramm — maintenance and repair specialist

**Primary spaces:** Back Lane, Ride Court, Observation Wheel, House of Mirrors.  
**Role:** shared mechanical/structural specialist for fair equipment that is beyond an operator's ordinary adjustment.

Hettie is not the boss of Emery Wicks or Tilda Fen. Operators decide ordinary operation and safe boarding within their authority; Hettie diagnoses, repairs and documents equipment problems.

Useful domains include:

- bearings, fasteners, drive/linkage wear and ordinary ride faults;
- lamps/wiring or equivalent final-world service technology where supported;
- warped frames, loose panels and structural issues in the House of Mirrors;
- distinguishing an ordinary optical/mechanical cause from `I cannot reproduce that`;
- repair parts, recent work and maintenance history she actually performed or reviewed.

### F-05 boundary

Hettie gives F-05 a crucial mundane investigative route. If shown the reported late reflection, she can inspect frame, glass, lighting, sightline and nearby mechanical causes.

She is **not** an oracle for the supernatural. `I found no mechanical cause` is a bounded finding, not proof that an impossible event occurred.

### Knowledge boundary

Hettie knows equipment she inspected, repair work, current maintenance requests and recurring faults. She does not know private carriage conversations, hidden historical records, every vendor's stock or the true nature of a phenomenon merely because it happened near machinery.

### Expected day

- PREOPEN: Back Lane with inspection rounds through Ride Court / wheel / mirrors;
- OPENING/LATE-MORNING: roving maintenance, strongest presence around Ride Court and House of Mirrors;
- MIDDAY/AFTERNOON: Back Lane staging plus incident-driven calls;
- DUSK: lighting/mechanical recheck before evening load;
- EVENING: on-call from Back Lane with visible travel to actual faults;
- CLOSING/AFTER-HOURS: shutdown repairs and deferred work.

## 15. Sella Birch — artisan and craft seller

**Primary space:** Market Row.  
**Secondary spaces:** Exhibition Yard during craft demonstration; Food Row / Dance Pavilion after closing her stall on selected evenings.  
**Role:** dedicated craft seller who gives Market Row a materially different identity from Vera Tallow's curiosities.

Sella makes and sells **current, honestly represented craft**, including small carved fair creatures, carved household objects, simple stamped ornaments, repaired handles/toys and limited annual pieces.

She is especially useful because she can discuss **how something was made** without automatically knowing **when, by whom or why** an old object was made.

### Provenance boundary

If shown the old stamped pond token from F-09, Sella may identify things such as tool marks, casting/stamping method, material, wear inconsistent with a modern souvenir, or similarities to current craft practice. She cannot certify the token's historical provenance solely from craftsmanship.

That preserves the evidence chain:

physical object -> craft/material observations -> current Fair Office material -> Ephraim/Vera claims -> older House of Records sources.

### Knowledge boundary

Sella knows her own materials, stock, prices, tools, customers she remembers, other artisans she actually knows and craft methods she recognizes. She separates `I made this`, `I know this method`, `this looks older than my work` and `I have no idea where it came from`.

### Expected day

- PREOPEN: Market Row setup;
- OPENING through AFTERNOON: Market Row sales, with one authored Exhibition Yard demonstration window where useful;
- DUSK: begins packing earlier than evening businesses;
- EVENING: stall normally closed; may appear as an off-duty visitor at food/music;
- CLOSING/AFTER-HOURS: packs through Back Lane and leaves.

## 16. Pella Wren — young recurring visitor

**Primary spaces:** Games Row, Fair Office & Prize Hall, Food Row, Ride Court.  
**Role:** the young visitor in F-02, `Thirty-Nine Tickets`.

Pella is not a morality prop. She has her own priorities, can change her mind about prizes and may care far more about a ridiculous 40-ticket object than any adult thinks reasonable.

At the F-02 moment she has **39 legitimate tickets** and wants a **40-ticket prize**. Her ticket count is physical/current state, not a scripted speech that remains 39 forever after the situation changes.

She may accept one legitimate transferable ticket, choose another prize, decide to earn one more if a suitable booth remains open, become frustrated, or leave without the desired object. Nell Harrow's rule remains 39 != 40.

### Knowledge boundary

Pella knows her own tickets, what she played, what she wants, family plans she was told and things she personally saw. Child confidence is not omniscience. Exaggeration and imperfect retelling remain attributed to her.

### Expected day

Usually arrives with Tobin Wren around OPENING/LATE-MORNING, moves through games/food/rides, reaches the Prize Hall naturally after earning tickets and normally leaves before deep evening unless a specifically authored family event keeps them later.

## 17. Tobin Wren — Pella's father and family continuity anchor

**Primary route:** Entrance -> Games/Food/Ride Court -> Prize Hall -> Grand/Market -> exit, varying by visit.  
**Role:** recurring family character who makes Pella part of an actual family visit rather than an isolated child quest token.

Tobin can be amused, tired, distracted, protective or embarrassed by negotiation at the prize counter. He does not automatically solve F-02 with money or pressure Nell to break the rules.

He may offer practical options, let Pella decide, or simply wait while she works through the disappointment.

### Knowledge boundary

Tobin knows his family, their current plans, purchases and things he personally witnessed. He does not speak for Pella's private thoughts and Pella can disagree with him.

### Continuity

On repeat fairs, the Wren family can remember prior rides, prizes, food, minor mishaps and people they actually met. They need not replay F-02 every year.

## 18. Lysa Marr — recurring adult visitor

**Primary spaces:** Food Row, Dance Pavilion, Observation Wheel, Pond Path.  
**Role:** one half of a recurring adult couple with life independent of Mara and the Adventurer.

Lysa likes dancing and evening fair atmosphere, has strong opinions about music and food, and is skeptical of the shell game. She may arrive before, with or after Tavin Roe depending on the state of their own plans.

She can remember Orin Bell, a recurring song, a prior wheel ride or an argument from an earlier fair when broader recurrence/memory authority legitimately supports that continuity.

## 19. Tavin Roe — recurring adult visitor

**Primary spaces:** Observation Wheel, Fishing Pond/Pond Path, Food Row, Dance Pavilion.  
**Role:** Lysa Marr's partner and an independently authored fairgoer.

Tavin enjoys the wheel and low-key pond-side wandering more than crowded dancing, but will dance sometimes because he actually chooses to. He may fish casually without being the derby specialist.

He and Lysa may flirt, disagree, split up for an activity, reunite, leave at different times, apologize, celebrate or simply have an ordinary evening. Their relationship is not a mirror built to teach the player how to behave with Mara.

### Couple law

Lysa and Tavin have **separate** knowledge, inventories, preferences and location state.

Do not model them as one `COUPLE` object. Either can be present without the other. A public proposal, argument or dance can be witnessed if it actually occurs in context, but private conversation is not automatically overheard or archived.

Their exact long-horizon relationship arc remains authored continuity work, not RNG.

## 20. Cassa Reed — rival angler

**Primary spaces:** Fishing Pond, Pond Path, Fair Office & Prize Hall during verified results.  
**Role:** recurring competitive angler who is neither official nor romance-protected.

Cassa knows the pond through repeated experience and has specific habits about bait, position and patience. Her advice can be useful but biased by personal preference and competitive motive.

She can:

- enter the derby independently;
- submit a verified fish;
- beat the Adventurer;
- beat Mara;
- lose without turning into a villain;
- remember prior public results;
- tease or challenge another angler;
- release a good fish even when somebody else would keep it, according to authored preference.

Silas Dace remains the official. Cassa cannot verify her own record.

### Knowledge boundary

Cassa knows catches she made or witnessed, pond patterns she personally learned, rumors from other anglers and current public results. She does not know the hidden RNG table, exact spawn weights or House of Records contents she never reviewed.

### Expected day

- PREOPEN/OPENING: usually absent or arriving;
- LATE-MORNING/MIDDAY: active at Fishing Pond / alternate banks;
- AFTERNOON: derby and weigh-in if entered;
- DUSK: Pond Path, result discussion or departure;
- EVENING: usually off duty as a visitor or gone; no guaranteed late schedule.

## Family and adult-social population law

These named visitors coexist with authored crowd population. Their existence does not require naming every nearby child, spouse, friend or parent.

Named visitors should be persistent enough to support recognition and continuity, but they are still visitors. They may leave early, skip an attraction, miss a fair day or be elsewhere on the grounds.

## No dedicated constable in the initial named roster

A fair-specific constable/safety officer is **not required at this planning stage**.

Ordinary concerns are already covered by:

- operators controlling their own equipment;
- Hettie handling repair/diagnosis;
- Berrin coordinating association-level incidents;
- Ada handling current incident paperwork;
- broader world authority for serious violence/property consequences where applicable.

Add a dedicated safety/constable character later only if an actual behavior/authority gap appears. Do not create one merely because real fairs often have security.

## Transient vendors remain deliberately open

The fair may later gain one or more named merchants/performers who attend only selected fairs. This pass does not name them because no current story/system requires one yet.

Vendor absence should eventually be coherent fair-state variation, not random deletion of a required service.

## Roster boundary

The named planning roster is now 20 people. That is enough to support a dense fair without turning the project into a census.

New names should require a concrete role, knowledge boundary, continuity need or story function. Background crowd life remains crowd life until there is a reason to promote somebody into persistent authored state.
