## Resolved in planning

The following are no longer open:

- **Public/common name:** The Flathead Fair.
- **Canonical name anchor:** the Flathead Mountains named at `CANYON-VIEW` in `1dungeon.zil`.
- **Primary physical attachment:** new northeast spur from canonical `CLEARING` to `FAIR-ROAD` and new fairground geography.
- **Existing surface barriers:** preserved; the fair does not clear the machete, impenetrable-forest, or storm-tossed-tree boundaries.
- **Off-season ground:** remains physically explorable when calendar state supports it; the fair does not vanish.
- **Recurrence in world fiction:** annual.
- **Institutional shape:** older regional fair association plus later Frobozz-branded sponsorship/concessions; not an all-Frobozz monoculture.
- **General admission:** free; individual rides, games, food, goods, rentals, and selected premium events use zorkmids.
- **Everyday zorkmid scale:** authored Highly Extended purchasing-power bands are stable; original Zork I score values are not treated as currency conversion.
- **Prize tickets:** earned redemption currency only; not ordinary money and not convertible back into zorkmids.
- **Signature elephant-ear baseline:** cinnamon-sugar 5 zm, with the initial menu/prices in `FAIR_PRICE_BOOK.md`.
- **First partial-identification drink:** `large drink` = pear-lime fizz, 4 zm.
- **Initial named core roster:** 12 working named NPCs with explicit knowledge boundaries in `FAIR_NPC_ROSTER.md`.

See the owning authorities rather than re-deriving these decisions from this ledger.
