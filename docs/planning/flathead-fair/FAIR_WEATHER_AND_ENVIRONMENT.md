# Flathead Fair weather and environment

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Dependency

The fair should compose the authored time/weather authority being developed in Release 1307 rather than inventing a second forecast or climate engine.

## Weather effects

Weather may alter:

- attendance/crowd prose;
- fishing conditions;
- ride availability;
- wind-sensitive rides;
- vendor covers/awnings;
- mud/puddles/ground condition;
- demand for hot/cold food;
- performance relocation/cancellation;
- nighttime visibility and lighting;
- Mara/NPC preferences and reactions.

## Rain law

Rain should not simply produce `FAIR CANCELLED`. Some activities move under cover, some rides close, vendors adapt, crowds compress, puddles/mud appear, and the fair acquires a different mood.

## Severe weather

Serious weather may cause authored safety responses. It must never become arbitrary disaster roulette or create an unrecoverable canonical failure.

## Time of day

Dusk and night should transform the fair through lights, performers, social population, sound, privacy, and closed stalls rather than only swapping one room-description sentence.
