# Flathead Fair NPC roster

**Status:** OPEN

## Population model

Use two layers:

1. **authored crowd population** for scale, motion, sound, incidental reactions, families, couples, children, and background activity;
2. **named NPCs** for persistent schedules, knowledge, inventory, motives, dialogue, quests, relationships, and repeat visits.

Initial long-term target: roughly **15–25 substantial named fair NPCs**, introduced as design needs justify them rather than all at once.

## Roles to fill

- fair manager / concession authority;
- information/ticket worker;
- observation-wheel operator;
- carousel/ride operator;
- fishing master / weigh-in official;
- rival anglers;
- prize clerk;
- elephant-ear/confection vendor;
- drink vendor;
- cook;
- honest game operator;
- dubious game operator;
- artisan;
- curiosity merchant;
- performer/musician;
- dance-pavilion figure;
- repair/maintenance worker;
- constable or fair safety authority if lore supports it;
- recurring elderly fairgoer;
- child/family NPCs for discovered errands;
- regular couple(s) whose history can change across fairs.

## Roster law

Do not create dozens of interchangeable named people. Named NPCs exist because they have knowledge, behavior, continuity, or a useful story role.
