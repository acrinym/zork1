# Flathead Fair adult social, romance, and intimacy

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Product law

The fair is not exclusively a child/family attraction. It is also an adult social place with dating, dancing, couples, flirting, quiet conversation, affection, evening venues, proposals, arguments, reconciliation, and established relationships.

## Contextual spaces

Useful social/privacy gradients include:

- crowded midway;
- dance pavilion;
- evening food/drink pavilion;
- observation-wheel carriage;
- pond path;
- overlook/hill;
- edge of a performance area;
- nearly empty grounds near closing.

## Relationship behavior

For Mara and other adult couples, fair behavior may include:

- asking someone to dance;
- sitting close;
- holding hands;
- kissing/affection;
- private conversation;
- teasing and flirting;
- gifts;
- anniversaries or proposals;
- leaving together for a more private location.

The fair itself supplies context. Broader relationship authorities govern consent, privacy, intimacy, boundaries, and character-specific willingness.

## Public context

The same affectionate behavior can draw different reactions in a quiet area versus blocking a pie-judging table. Social reaction should be contextual, not moralistic.

## Other relationships

The fair should contain other couples and social histories so romance does not exist only around Mara. Returning NPC couples may change over later fairs.

## Boundary

No fair-specific sex minigame, no `ROMANCE LEVEL` gate, and no scripted intimacy that overrides autonomy or consent.
