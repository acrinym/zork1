# Flathead Fair rewards, prizes, and collectibles

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Prize economy

Midway games/contests may award fair tickets, ribbons, trophies, or direct prizes. Normal products remain purchasable with zorkmids.

## Prize tiers

### Small

Whistles, ribbons, candy, cheap toys, novelty hats, tiny souvenirs.

### Good

Stuffed creatures (including a stuffed grue), decorative objects, clothing, books, music boxes, fishing gear, useful but nonessential curiosities.

### Rare

Annual items, artisan pieces, unusual property decorations, unique clothing, limited memorabilia, strange objects with later relevance.

### Prestige

Extremely difficult bragging-rights objects or records. These must never be required for canonical completion.

## Physical persistence

A won object is the real object. If it is given to Mara, stored at property, lost, damaged, or carried elsewhere, later fair logic should refer to that actual history rather than silently spawning a replacement.

## Annual collecting

Recurring fairs may have dated/year-specific ribbons, shirts, mugs, programs, or other memorabilia when broader time authority can support the chronology.

## Anti-grind

Prize progression should reward variety, records, discovery, and difficult accomplishments more than repeatedly farming one trivial booth.
