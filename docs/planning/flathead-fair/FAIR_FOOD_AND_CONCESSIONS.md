# Flathead Fair food and concessions

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Signature law

**Elephant ears are mandatory.** They are a recognizable staple of the Flathead Fair and should contribute smell, taste, commerce, sharing, memory, and comedy.

## Working elephant-ear menu

Planning prices only:

- plain: `4 zm`;
- cinnamon-sugar: `5 zm`;
- apple-topped: `6 zm`;
- honey/nut: `7 zm`;
- absurd Frobozz-deluxe variant: around `9 zm`;
- oversized sharing version: around `12 zm`.

Final prices depend on economy calibration.

## Food object behavior

Food/drink should support reasonable authored actions:

- examine;
- smell;
- taste/lick;
- eat/drink;
- share/tear portions where appropriate;
- give;
- carry/container interactions;
- drop/throw where the normal object authority permits;
- spoil/cool/melt only where bounded and worth the complexity.

## Other staples

Candy apples, spun sugar, roasted nuts, fried dough variants, pies, pastries, savory fair food, fruit, sweets, cider/lemonade/fizz, specialty drinks, souvenir vessels, and regional foods appropriate to final lore.

## Product specificity

`large drink` may be a temporary description, never the final internal design. Every sellable prepared item should have an authored flavor/identity that knowledgeable characters can discuss.

## Sharing and memory

Food can become a relationship memory without numerical romance points. Example: a later fair can recall an argument over who ate most of the first shared elephant ear.

## Vendor expertise

Concession staff know their ingredients, prices, menu, preparation, shortages, and recommendations unless a specific story gives them reason not to.
