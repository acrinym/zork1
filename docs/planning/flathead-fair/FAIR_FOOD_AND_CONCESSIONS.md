# Flathead Fair food and concessions

**Status:** STABLE FOR PLANNING

## Signature law

**Elephant ears are mandatory.** They are a recognizable staple of the Flathead Fair and contribute smell, taste, commerce, sharing, memory, and comedy.

## Locked elephant-ear baseline

Initial planning menu:

- plain: `4 zm`;
- **cinnamon-sugar: `5 zm`** — signature baseline;
- apple-topped: `6 zm`;
- honey/nut: `7 zm`;
- absurd Frobozz-deluxe variant: `9 zm`;
- oversized sharing version: `12 zm`.

These prices now follow the fair's stable purchasing-power model in `FAIR_PRODUCTS_AND_ZORKMID_ECONOMY.md` and `FAIR_PRICE_BOOK.md`.

The exact vendor name, recipe details, frying vessel, serving paper, and individual product prose remain to be authored with the vendor roster.

## First product-identification test case

The first canonical planning example for contextual product knowledge is:

- player-facing initial description: **`large drink`**;
- actual identity: **pear-lime fizz**;
- price: **4 zm**;
- basic seller answer to flavor question: **pear and lime, mostly pear**.

This deliberately ordinary product exists to prove that generic presentation does not imply generic world knowledge.

A player may learn its identity through signage, asking, smell/taste, another character, or prior memory. Different NPCs know different subsets of the facts.

## Food object behavior

Food/drink should support reasonable authored actions:

- examine;
- smell;
- taste/lick;
- eat/drink;
- share/tear portions where appropriate;
- give;
- carry/container interactions;
- drop/throw where normal object authority permits;
- spoil/cool/melt only where bounded and worth the complexity.

## Other staples

Initial price-book staples now include candy apples, spun sugar, roasted nuts, pickles, seasonal fruit, savory hand pies, meat pies, fried potatoes, sausage rolls, pie slices, lemon drinks, cider, hot spiced cider, pear-lime fizz, and souvenir fair mugs.

This is an opening product roster, not a final maximum menu.

## Product specificity

`large drink`, `fried thing`, `red sweet`, and similar descriptions may represent incomplete player knowledge. Every sellable prepared item still has a concrete authored identity.

Knowledgeable characters should be able to discuss relevant facts such as:

- flavor;
- ingredients;
- notable allergens/ingredients;
- sweetness/spice/sourness;
- serving temperature;
- preparation;
- price;
- freshness;
- who made it;
- whether they personally like it.

## Sharing and memory

Food can become relationship memory without numerical romance points. A later fair can remember who bought the first elephant ear, what Mara liked, whether they shared it, and the entirely objective historical controversy over who ate most of it.

## Vendor expertise

Concession staff know their own ingredients, prices, menu, preparation, shortages, and recommendations unless a specific story gives them reason not to.

A vendor may not know every other stall's recipe. Correct bounded ignorance is part of the design.
