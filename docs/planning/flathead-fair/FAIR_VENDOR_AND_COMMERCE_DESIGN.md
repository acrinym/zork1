# Flathead Fair vendor and commerce design

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Vendor standard

A vendor is an NPC with product expertise, schedule, stock, personality, and local knowledge, not a vending machine with dialogue attached.

Vendors should understand:

- what they sell;
- prices and sizes;
- ingredients/materials where appropriate;
- preparation or maker information;
- sold-out items and substitutions;
- specials;
- basic neighboring-stall directions;
- their own opinions and sales pitch.

## Merchant diversity

The fair should mix:

- independent food sellers;
- artisans;
- recurring family vendors;
- temporary traveling merchants;
- Frobozz-branded concessions where useful;
- suspicious game operators;
- honest operators;
- curiosity dealers;
- performers selling programs/merchandise.

## Stock behavior

Some inventory can vary by fair day or year, but stock variation must be authored and bounded. Rare stock should be discoverable without requiring save-scumming.

## Conversation as navigation

Players should be able to ask useful questions such as where to find cider, fishing bait, a particular attraction, another vendor, or a missing item. The fair should be navigable socially, not only from memorized exits.

## Bargaining

Bargaining may exist for appropriate merchants, especially curiosities or secondhand goods. It should not become a universal haggling stat applied to fixed concession prices.

## Vendor memory

Returning merchants may remember purchases, favors, arguments, records, or repeat customers when existing persistence authority supports it.
