# Flathead Fair miniquests, incidents, and rumors

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Discovery law

Most fair objectives should be discovered through observation, conversation, signs, events, or objects rather than dumped into a universal quest log.

## Story families

### Mundane help

- recover a lost purse;
- retrieve a balloon/object;
- find a missing crate;
- deliver food to an overworked operator;
- help someone locate another visitor.

### Competition

- catch a particular or largest fish;
- beat a record;
- win a ribbon;
- perform well across several different game types.

### Social

- help a nervous visitor attempt an attraction;
- resolve or clarify a misunderstanding;
- reunite people who keep missing one another.

### Investigation

- determine whether a game is rigged;
- identify why a mirror behaves incorrectly;
- investigate missing food/stock;
- understand a recurring after-hours sound or event.

### Zork-weird

- an object appears where it should not;
- an NPC remembers an apparently impossible fair detail;
- a harmless activity exposes a deeper world fact.

## Incident law

Minor random incidents may vary, but authored one-time stories need stable causes and consequences. Do not build an incident generator.

## Rewards

Not every story needs money or tickets. Information, reputation, a relationship change, a physical object, access, a memory, or merely a good joke may be the reward.
