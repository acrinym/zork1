# Flathead Fair Mara experience

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Goal

The fair should become one of the strongest places for the Adventurer and Mara to simply live together for a while. It must not become a relationship minigame.

## Mara agency

Mara may:

- suggest an attraction;
- refuse one;
- ask to return to something she likes;
- buy food or merchandise for herself;
- buy something for the Adventurer where appropriate;
- wander toward an attraction/vendor;
- fish or enter a contest independently;
- beat the Adventurer;
- get hungry, amused, bored, tired, annoyed, excited, or curious;
- initiate dancing or affection when relationship/context supports it;
- remember prior fairs and shared incidents.

## Preferences

Preferences should be authored and stable enough to feel like character, not random likes/dislikes. Price does not determine emotional value.

Potential domains:

- rides/heights;
- fishing;
- dancing/music;
- foods and drinks;
- particular games;
- crowds;
- curiosities;
- gifts;
- nighttime fair atmosphere.

## Shared memories

Candidates include:

- first fair together;
- first observation-wheel ride;
- first dance;
- a ridiculous game failure;
- a fish/record;
- a storm/rain incident;
- staying until closing;
- a gift won or bought;
- a food-sharing dispute, including who actually ate most of an elephant ear.

## No DATE MODE

The player visits the fair with Mara through ordinary world actions. Relationship-appropriate behavior emerges from existing autonomy, consent, mood, memory, and location authorities.
