# Flathead Fair Mara experience

**Status:** DESIGNING

## Goal

The fair should become one of the strongest places for the Adventurer and Mara to simply live together for a while. It must not become a relationship minigame.

## Mara agency

Mara may:

- suggest an attraction;
- refuse one;
- ask to return to something she likes;
- buy food or merchandise for herself;
- buy something for the Adventurer where appropriate;
- wander toward an attraction/vendor;
- fish or enter a contest independently;
- beat the Adventurer;
- get hungry, amused, bored, tired, annoyed, excited, or curious;
- initiate dancing or affection when relationship/context supports it;
- remember prior fairs and shared incidents.

## Preferences

Preferences should be authored and stable enough to feel like character, not random likes/dislikes. Price does not determine emotional value.

Potential domains:

- rides/heights;
- fishing;
- dancing/music;
- foods and drinks;
- particular games;
- crowds;
- curiosities;
- gifts;
- nighttime fair atmosphere.

## Shared memories

Candidates include:

- first fair together;
- first observation-wheel ride;
- first dance;
- a ridiculous game failure;
- a fish/record;
- a storm/rain incident;
- staying until closing;
- a gift won or bought;
- a food-sharing dispute, including who actually ate most of an elephant ear.

These are **personal lived memories**. They do not automatically become Fair Office paperwork or House of Records entries.

A public fact may independently create documentary evidence. Examples:

- Mara wins a public derby, so a verified result can exist;
- she earns a public ribbon or record;
- she is a named witness in an incident report because she actually gives a statement.

That documentary fact remains separate from private experience around it. A public wheel ticket does not archive the conversation inside the carriage. A contest result does not archive how she felt about winning.

See `FAIR_HALL_OF_RECORDS_INTEGRATION.md` and `FAIR_PERSISTENCE_AND_MEMORY.md`.

## Recurrence

Later fairs should let Mara remember selected prior experiences without turning her into a transcript database.

Useful recurrence can include:

- preferring or avoiding a previously tried attraction;
- recognizing a vendor she actually met;
- remembering a gift or prize still physically owned;
- recalling a song, fish, storm, argument, joke or closing-night moment;
- noticing that a ride/vendor/record changed since the prior fair.

Memory should be semantic and character-specific.

## No DATE MODE

The player visits the fair with Mara through ordinary world actions. Relationship-appropriate behavior emerges from existing autonomy, consent, mood, memory, and location authorities.

There is no fair-only relationship mode, no automatic affection reward for completing attractions, and no institutional surveillance of private Mara/Adventurer life.
