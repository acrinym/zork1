## Release 1276 invariant

Mara's usefulness comes from **her actual knowledge**: what she witnessed, recovered, mapped, was told, handled, or independently discovered. She is a mappist, so her real route/map knowledge becomes field guidance. She must never become an omniscient hint terminal.

Their physical behavior, odd placement, inventory burden, persistent dropped location, and exposure to real world hazards create uncertainty and expedition logistics. Some may become useful later through emergent authored composition. Some may remain junk forever.

The explicitly approved starting catalog and detailed boundaries live in the Release 1277 planning contract.
