# Highly Extended Zork — Adventurer's Guide

This is the player document **outside the game**. It is not a map and not a walkthrough. It tells you what kind of world you are walking into.

The in-game leaflet in the mailbox is still an advertisement. It now also names this edition honestly. For how to play a parser game at all, start with [`expanded/README.md`](../expanded/README.md). For what the current story file actually is, use this page.

**Current locked story on `master` (after this train merges):** Release 1295 — West-of-House Described Nouns.

**Play the Glulx story**, not a random `.z3` in the historical collection, unless you mean to play original Infocom Zork I.

---

## What to expect

You type short English commands. The game answers in prose. There is no illustrated map, no point-and-click GUI, and no AI companion inventing rooms.

**If a room already named a thing, you should be able to examine it.** That is the live product law. `You can't see any tree here!` after the room just mentioned trees is a lie; those lies are being closed on purpose.

**RECAP is earned.** A fresh game does not dump House-of-Records architecture, dossiers, or regional files. Recap names what you have actually visited or caused.

**The white house is not an inn.** Rest, jars, windows, and boards have physical consequences. Sleeping in the wrong place can still be fatal. The house can be dangerous even when it looks merely boarded.

**Canonical Zork puzzles still work.** Lamp, mailbox, kitchen window, trophy case, thief, troll, dam, boat, cyclops hunger, and the rest of the 1980 spine remain the authorities they were. Highly Extended adds more real nouns and more honest refusals; it does not replace those solutions with a skill tree.

**Mara, if you meet her, is one authored person.** She is not a romance meter, a generic follower framework, or an omniscient hint AI.

**Combat and darkness still kill.** There is no player-facing invulnerability menu. Developer survey flags, when they exist, are not part of a normal play.

## The leaflet

`READ LEAFLET` still opens with Infocom's "WELCOME TO ZORK!" advertisement (adventure, danger, low cunning). It now adds that this copy is Highly Extended: described places are usually examinable, recap is earned, and the house is not a safe inn.

It will never become a spoiler sheet or a command list. This guide is the longer outside-the-game companion.

---

## Opening commands that should already mean something

At West of House the look text names wild grass, weathered boards, and settled silence. You can examine those. The mailbox, leaflet, and boarded door remain the opening objects they always were.

North of the house, the trees the room names are examinable. The climbable tree that leads Up a Tree is still on Forest Path, not on every tree.

---

## What this is not

- Not a map of the underground.
- Not a list of treasures or points.
- Not a substitute for typing `LOOK` and believing the paragraph you get.
- Not a GUI, illustration layer, or chatbot.

If the game's own sentences and this guide disagree, the game's sentences win — and the guide should be corrected.
