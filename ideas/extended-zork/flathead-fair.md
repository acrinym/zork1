# Flathead Fair organ (Highly Extended)

Concept source for Release **1310**. Planning authority: `docs/planning/flathead-fair/`. Implementation: `glulx/flathead-fair/`. Train: `.beads/cursor_zork_flathead_fair.beadtrain`.

Still Zork. Optional. Parser-first. No illustrated frontend. No carnival engine. No DATE MODE.

## Player-facing whole

The Adventurer can walk northeast from canonical `CLEARING` onto a packed public road and into a recurring Flathead Fair. General admission is free. Canonical N/E/S/W from the clearing are unchanged. The White House, Dam, and underground remain solvable without attending.

On the grounds: sixteen public rooms plus a five-node House of Mirrors and a wheel carriage. Twenty named people with bounded knowledge. Zorkmids for food, drinks, rides, and games. Prize tickets are not money. Nell does not round: 39 is not 40. Eight Games Row booths are eight machines, not one RNG in eight signs. Kester's tack-wax is inspectable evidence. Silas verifies fish; Cassa competes. Mara attends as herself. Tomas's large drink is pear-lime fizz (ask him). Dusk opens The Lantern Table. Wind can close the Association wheel with a fare refund. Current paper lives with Ada; older programs live in the existing House of Records, not a second archive.
