# Mara Part 2 — Field Capability Discovery

**Release target:** 1259  
**Branch:** `agent/mara-field-capability-discovery-20260814`  
**Parent:** qualified Release 1258 / PR #63  
**Status:** implementation train

## Product thesis

Mara does not gain a skill tree.

She lives through situations that reveal what Mara Tallow can actually do.

The governing chain is:

> existing experience → pressured improvisation → unexpected success or failure → Mara's own appraisal → remembered capability → deliberate later reuse

A capability is not real merely because a flag says it exists. The first discovery must happen in authored physical play, and at least one later situation must show Mara choosing to reuse what she learned.

## Why this is Mara

Mara already has the ingredients:

- field-survey mathematics;
- practical knowledge of anchors, loads, grades, distance, and route geometry;
- a measured waxed field rope she actually owns;
- a habit of listening to machinery, water, stone, and architectural evidence;
- real recent bodily history from the Dam ladder;
- an independent professional identity that predates the Adventurer.

Release 1259 asks what happens when skills Mara thinks of as **survey work** escape the notebook and turn out to live in her body and perception too.

This is adventurous competence without making Mara a Lara Croft clone, superhero, generic party member, or player-controlled stunt machine.

## Chapter 1 — recovery is not erasure

Release 1258's Dam injury remains a permanent fact in Mara's biography.

If Mara has actually chosen the House as a base and is physically staying there, a completed existing House full-rest cycle can advance her current bodily state from injured to recovered.

Recovery means:

- the shoulder moves freely again;
- the palm has healed into a visible scar;
- the original ladder injury remains true and queryable;
- no retroactive rewrite says the injury never happened.

This matters mechanically: an unrecovered shoulder can be a concrete reason Mara refuses a high-load swing she can correctly understand on paper.

## Chapter 2 — the Frigid pendulum

At the Dam Base, a fixed old service pipe crosses above a narrow side channel of the River Frigid. Across that cold water, an old brass survey plummet is visible where it was left on a maintenance lip.

Mara can see the geometry before she knows whether she can execute it.

If her shoulder is still injured, she refuses the stunt for a bodily reason rather than because a capability flag is absent.

Once recovered, with her exact field rope in her own custody, asking Mara about the river can lead **her** to decide to try:

1. inspect the service pipe as a real anchor;
2. calculate the rope length and return arc from measured marks;
3. commit to the swing herself;
4. clear the cold channel;
5. retrieve the real brass plummet;
6. swing back to the same landing.

The first success should surprise Mara too:

> "I knew the numbers," Mara says after a moment. "I did not know I could do that."

No perk appears. No XP is awarded. What exists is one thing Mara actually did.

## Chapter 3 — deliberate movement reuse becomes a second discovery

At the Deep Canyon, an old fixed ringbolt and a reachable lateral shelf create a second authored use of the same movement principle.

This time Mara is not surprised by the rope swing itself. She deliberately reuses the technique she learned at the Frigid.

While on the shelf she strikes the recovered brass plummet against stone to test the wall and listens to the returns.

The surprising part is now perceptual.

Years of estimating voids, watercourses, gallery dimensions, and structural hollows have trained her ear more deeply than she realized. The returns separate in a way she can interpret as distance and shape.

Her own appraisal can land on something like:

> "I thought I was estimating," Mara says. "I was ranging it."

This records two distinct facts:

- pendulum movement deliberately reused;
- acoustic ranging newly discovered.

## Chapter 4 — acoustic ranging deliberately reused

Mara does **not** solve the canonical Loud Room puzzle for the player.

The room becomes eligible for her expedition only after the canonical room is already in a state where she can safely enter and think. Release 1259 must not bypass the existing `ECHO` solution or the already-shipped hearing-protection workaround.

Once the room is quiet enough and Mara has already discovered acoustic ranging, she can follow the Adventurer into the Loud Room.

Asking her about acoustics there lets her deliberately perform a second measurement with the brass plummet. The result is useful field knowledge about the chamber's shape and passage returns, but it does not open a secret door, award treasure, or auto-solve a canonical puzzle.

That later reuse is the proof that the first canyon discovery became knowledge Mara can intentionally carry forward.

## Capability biography facts

Release 1259 should advance Mara to schema 7 with named facts, not generic skill levels:

- `ladder-recovered`
- `pendulum-discovered`
- `pendulum-reused`
- `acoustic-discovered`
- `acoustic-reused`
- `plummet-recovered`

These facts answer concrete historical questions. They are not an extensible RPG stats framework.

## Player-facing helpfulness

The point is not merely to watch Mara perform tricks.

Her capabilities should change what shared expeditions can reasonably do:

- a recovered Mara can again volunteer for strenuous authored field work;
- pendulum technique lets her retrieve evidence from physically awkward positions without teleportation;
- acoustic ranging lets her produce bounded, useful spatial observations in authored rooms;
- a discovered capability can expand a route Mara is personally willing and able to join;
- her body, equipment custody, environment, and prior history still determine whether she acts.

## Hard boundaries

Release 1259 does **not** create:

- XP;
- levels;
- perks;
- a skill menu;
- random stat rolls;
- a generic companion-ability API;
- universal traversal AI;
- generic rope physics;
- a generic acoustics simulator;
- automatic puzzle solutions;
- an omniscient hint engine;
- superhero abilities;
- player-issued remote-control stunt commands;
- retroactive healing that erases prior injury;
- a Lara Croft or Indiana Jones imitation.

The rope is allowed to remain suspiciously useful. 😏

But the subject is Mara, not rope.
